A pointer catches the element on its way only while it visibly moves (more than 2px from where it's headed, or faster than 40px/s). In the last of a spring's tail it's put in place instead: a finger on a sheet that has as good as stopped scrolls its list with the list's own momentum, where it used to be caught and scroll the list without any.
